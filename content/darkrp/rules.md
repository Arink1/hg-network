These are the general rules. Job specific rules are in the F4 menu for each job. Staff decide punishments. Appeals go through a ticket in Discord.

## 1. RDM and NLR

- Do not kill without a valid roleplay reason. Being annoyed is not a reason.
- New Life Rule: after you die you forget the last five minutes and you cannot return to where you died for five minutes.
- Revenge killing is RDM.

## 2. Raiding and mugging

- Advert raids, mugs, kidnaps and counters in chat before you act.
- Raid cooldown is 15 minutes per base. You cannot raid the same base twice in 30 minutes.
- Mug cap is $2,000. Give the victim ten seconds.
- No raiding if you can see the base has no one inside, and no raiding as a government job without a warrant.

## 3. Building

- No building during a raid. Building signs mean you cannot be raided and cannot have printers, guns or entities.
- Maximum three fading doors per entrance, no one way props, no crouch or jump entrances.
- Keep a keypad within two meters of every fading door, visible and working.

## 4. FailRP and metagaming

- Stay in character. Do not use information from out of character sources.
- No breaking character to argue rules. Use `@` to call staff.
- No prop abuse: prop climb, prop block, prop kill, prop surf.

## 5. Chat and behavior

- No slurs, harassment or doxxing. No advertising.
- Mic spam is a mute, then a ban.

## 6. Government jobs

- Police need a reason to arrest and must follow the procedure book.
- The mayor cannot make laws that target individual players or make the game unplayable.
- Corruption is allowed only as a roleplay with consequences, not as an excuse to RDM.
