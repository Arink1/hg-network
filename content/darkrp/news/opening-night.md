HGDarkRP opens **Saturday, September 5 at 7:00 PM ET**. The first 48 hours run as a soft launch: bans are shorter, we listen harder, and the economy may be tuned live.

## What is in

- 60 jobs across government, crime, business and civilian
- Player owned shops with stock, upkeep and a storefront sign
- A mayor election every two hours with real laws and a real budget
- Raid timers, a working court, and a police force with a procedure book

## Content pack

Subscribe to the workshop collection linked in Discord before Saturday. It is under 1.5 GB and it is the only download. Players without it will see errors and will be kicked after five minutes.
