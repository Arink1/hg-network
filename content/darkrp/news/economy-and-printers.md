Money on HGDarkRP comes in through salaries, printers, business sales and government contracts. It leaves through property tax, upkeep, raids and the casino. Here is how printers fit.

## Printers

- Four tiers. Each tier prints faster and is louder.
- Printers overheat. A cooler costs money and needs to be bought again after a raid.
- The government can scan for printers with a warrant. Warrants need a reason and a judge.

## Why this matters

A printer base should be a decision, not a default. If you run printers, you need a crew, doors, a cooler budget and a plan for the police. If you do not, you can make a comfortable living as a shop owner or a government worker and never touch one.

We will publish economy stats every Sunday in Discord and adjust when something runs away.
