---
title: HGCrafting rules
updated: 2026-08-20
---

Breaking a rule gets a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban depending on how bad it was. Staff decide. Appeals go through a ticket in Discord.

## 1. Play fair

- No hacked clients, x-ray, auto clickers or exploits. Mods that only change visuals (shaders, minimaps without cave mode) are fine.
- No duplication glitches. Report them, do not use them.
- No alt accounts to farm votes, claims or rewards.

## 2. Respect claims

- Do not grief, steal from or trap other players. Claims protect you, and unclaimed builds are still protected by this rule.
- PvP is on outside spawn but only with consent. Killing someone who did not agree to fight is a ban.
- Do not build within 100 blocks of another claim without asking.

## 3. Keep chat usable

- English in global chat. Any language in private messages.
- No slurs, harassment, politics or advertising other servers.
- No spam, caps lock walls or begging for items and ranks.

## 4. Do not abuse the economy

- No scamming. A trade or deal you agree to is binding.
- No price manipulation with alts.
- Refund requests for in-game trades are not handled by staff.

## 5. Staff decisions are final in the moment

Argue in a ticket, not in chat. Staff can be wrong, and tickets are how that gets fixed.
