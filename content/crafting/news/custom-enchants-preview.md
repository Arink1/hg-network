Custom enchants are the biggest change from vanilla survival on HGCrafting. A few ground rules before the list:

- Enchants drop from bosses, fishing, and the enchanter at spawn. None are sold in the store.
- Every enchant has a vanilla counter. Nothing is unblockable.
- Enchant books can be traded between players.

## Highlights

| Enchant | Item | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Telepathy | Tools | Mined blocks go straight to your inventory |
| Haste | Pickaxe | Mining speed, tiered |
| Lifesteal | Sword | Small heal on hit |
| Auto smelt | Pickaxe | Ores come out as ingots |
| Featherweight | Boots | No fall damage, tiered |
| Harvest | Hoe | Replants crops as you break them |

The full list with tiers is pinned in Discord under #enchants. Feedback on balance is welcome there before launch.
