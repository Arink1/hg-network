Season 1 of HGCrafting opens **Saturday, September 5 at 3:00 PM ET**. Here is what is waiting for you.

## The world

- 10,000 by 10,000 overworld border, Nether and End open from day one
- Spawn is a neutral market town. No building inside the spawn radius, shops are rented from the town board
- Hard difficulty, keep inventory off, phantoms off

## Claims

Every player starts with 200 claim blocks and earns 100 more per hour online, capped at 20,000. Claims protect blocks, containers and animals. Claim disputes go to a Helper, not to chat.

## Economy

Currency enters the world through jobs, selling to the server shop at spawn and weekly challenges. Ranks never grant money. Chest shops and the auction house are open from launch.

## Season length

Season 1 ends **December 1**. The top three on the season leaderboard get a permanent title on every future season. The full map goes into the archive for download after the reset.

See you Saturday. The address is `play.hgcrafting.com`.
