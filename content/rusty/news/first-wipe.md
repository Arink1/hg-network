HGRusty goes live with its first wipe on **Thursday, September 3 at 2:00 PM ET**.

## Settings

- 2x gather, 2x smelting, 2x crafting speed
- Default stack sizes, default loot tables, default decay
- Max group of 5, enforced with TC auth and base log checks
- Free starter kit on spawn: stone tools, a bow and 50 cloth
- Home teleport with a five minute cooldown, disabled while in combat or raid block

## Map

4000 size, procedural with custom monuments and a ring road. The map seed goes up in Discord one hour before wipe.

## Wipe schedule

- Map wipes every **Thursday at 2:00 PM ET**
- Blueprints wipe the **first Thursday of each month**, the same time as the map
- Forced Facepunch updates always wipe the map

Queue skip is the only monthly package in the store. Everything else is a cosmetic or a convenience, and the full list is on the store page.
