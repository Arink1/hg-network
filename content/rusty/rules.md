---
title: HGRusty rules
updated: 2026-08-20
---

Few rules, all enforced. Staff decide punishments. Appeals go through a ticket in Discord.

## 1. Group limit is five

- Five players maximum per group. A group is anyone you share a base, TC auth, codes, loot or a team with.
- Alliances count. If two groups share a compound, they are one group.
- Swapping members to stay at five is allowed once per wipe. Tell staff first.

## 2. No cheating

- No cheats, scripts, macros, ESP or exploits. Ban is permanent and applies across HG Network.
- No VPN evasion of a ban.
- Found a glitch? Report it. Using it is the same as cheating.

## 3. No griefing

- Do not wall off, foundation wipe or deliberately lock someone out of their base after a raid. Take the loot and leave.
- Do not build on or block roads, monuments or the map edge.
- No base designs that abuse stability glitches.

## 4. Chat

- English in global. No slurs, no harassment, no doxxing, no advertising.
- Trash talk is fine. Targeted harassment is not.

## 5. Staff

- Admins do not play on the server. If you see one, report it.
- Do not spam reports. Include a time and a location.
